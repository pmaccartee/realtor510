The Property

A Smithsonian-Listed Bayfront Compound

One of Point Richmond's most extraordinary residential offerings — where art, architecture, and nature converge.

This 5,027 square foot Bayfront compound represents a rare combination of architectural significance and natural beauty. Home to the nationally renowned Wave Garden — a Smithsonian-listed garden featuring curated sculptures and exceptional Bay views — this property was cherished by the same prominent family for over four decades before its sale.

Featured on Dwell.com, the residence seamlessly blends modern architectural elements with the natural beauty of its hillside setting. Mesmerizing views sweep across the San Francisco Bay while intimate spaces provide for both grand entertaining and quiet contemplation.

The residence spans two complete levels: the main floor features 3 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms with an open-plan living area and library. The garden level adds 3 more bedrooms, a full bathroom, and a complete second kitchen, living, and dining area — connected to all floors by elevator. A 7,000 square foot private park completes this singular offering.

The Location

Historic Point Richmond

A waterfront village with deep character and an emerging tech presence.

Tucked into the hills above historic Point Richmond, this residence sits in one of the Bay Area's most charming neighborhoods. Point Richmond offers a perfect blend of small-town character and urban sophistication — a village atmosphere that has attracted everyone from longtime residents to OpenAI, which recently leased a 202,000-square-foot facility on the waterfront, bringing an estimated 730 jobs to the area.

The walkable downtown centers around the historic Hotel Mac (built 1911) and its Italian restaurant Biancoverde, while the Masquers Playhouse — a community theater operating for over 60 years — anchors the cultural scene. Local institutions like Up & Under Pub and Little Louie's reflect the maritime heritage of this former company town.

With walkable scale, established character, and emerging economic drivers, Point Richmond represents one of the East Bay's most distinctive residential markets — authentic village life with genuine appreciation potential.
