Featured on Dwell.com, the residence seamlessly blends modern architectural elements with the natural beauty of its hillside setting. The property offers mesmerizing views across the San Francisco Bay while providing intimate spaces for both grand entertaining and quiet contemplation.

An elevator connects all floors, and the 7,000 square foot private park provides a truly unique setting in the East Bay market.

Location

Historic Point Richmond

A waterfront village with deep character and emerging tech presence

Tucked into the hills above historic Point Richmond, this unique residence sits in one of the Bay Area's most charming neighborhoods. Point Richmond offers a perfect blend of small-town character and urban sophistication — a village atmosphere that has attracted everyone from longtime residents to OpenAI, which recently leased a 202,000-square-foot facility on the waterfront, bringing an estimated 730 jobs to the area.

The neighborhood's walkable downtown centers around the historic Hotel Mac (built 1911) with its Italian restaurant Biancoverde, while the Masquers Playhouse — a community theater operating for over 60 years — anchors the cultural scene. Local institutions like Up & Under Pub and Little Louie's reflect the maritime heritage of this former company town, while the village's proximity to both San Francisco Ferry service and Richmond's expanding tech corridor creates a unique investment dynamic.

With its walkable scale, established character, and emerging economic drivers, Point Richmond represents one of the East Bay's most distinctive residential markets — authentic village life with genuine appreciation potential.

Market Context

Point Richmond's evolving investment landscape

Point Richmond's real estate market reflects both its historic character and emerging economic drivers. OpenAI's recent commitment to a 202,000-square-foot waterfront facility represents the largest tech investment in the area's modern history, with Mayor Eduardo Martinez projecting $75 million in annual economic benefits and 730 new jobs.

This institutional validation of Point Richmond as a tech-accessible location — combined with direct ferry service to San Francisco and established village character — creates a unique value proposition. Properties with architectural distinction and Bay views, like the Wave Garden Residence, represent the premium tier of this evolving market.

For sellers of distinctive homes, Point Richmond offers the rare combination of authentic character and genuine appreciation drivers — a market positioned for continued strength as the East Bay attracts tech workers seeking alternatives to traditional Peninsula commutes.
