The Oakland Hills rarely produces properties like this. Set behind a private gate on over half an acre, 4451 Saint Andrews delivers two fully self-contained living environments under one roof — and sweeping views of the Bay and the San Francisco skyline from the primary residence.

Sequoyah Hills is Oakland's quieter, more private Hills neighborhood — larger lots, longer driveways, and the particular sense of seclusion that comes from being above the city without feeling disconnected from it. Properties here trade less frequently than Montclair or Piedmont Avenue, and when a compound of this scale comes to market, it doesn't stay long.

4451 Saint Andrews sits at the top of a long private driveway behind a gated entrance. On over 0.65 acres, the lot feels genuinely expansive in a market where half-acre parcels in the Hills are increasingly rare.

"Two fully self-contained living environments, sweeping Bay views, a private gate, and over half an acre. Sold at $1,650,000 — strong execution in a market that rewards well-positioned properties."

The Result

This compound served a clear value proposition: a multigenerational family needing independent but connected living spaces, a buyer who wanted rental income from the lower unit, or someone who simply wanted the scale, privacy, and views that 4451 Saint Andrews delivers. At $1,650,000 for 4,593 square feet in Sequoyah Hills, the price per square foot represented exceptional value relative to Piedmont and upper Montclair comparables — and buyers recognized it.
