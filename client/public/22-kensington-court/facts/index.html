Location

Kensington is a small, unincorporated residential community in the Berkeley Hills, bordering Berkeley to the south and El Cerrito to the north. 22 Kensington Court sits off Cowper Avenue near Arlington Avenue.

  • Ye Olde School Trail runs next to the property: a wooded footpath toward Kensington Elementary.
  • Colusa Circle, Kensington's small village center, is nearby.
  • Solano Avenue (Albany/Berkeley) restaurants and shops are a short drive.
  • Tilden Regional Park and Wildcat Canyon Regional Park are minutes away.
